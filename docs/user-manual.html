01 Two systems, one set of records

Read this section before anything else. Almost every question about "why did that not appear" is answered here.

The website public

nationalbookofrecords.in and its admin panel. Applicants apply here, pay here, and verify their certificate here. It is the source of truth for anything the public can see.

The CRM internal

This system. Staff-only. It mirrors website applications and adds the work the website has no concept of: verification queues, tasks, sales calls, internal notes, reports.

The one rule that explains everything

An application only appears in the CRM once it has been approved on the website. Applications sitting at pending, under review or draft are deliberately not sent across. If a colleague says "I submitted it but it is not in the CRM", the answer is nearly always that nobody has approved it yet.

Which system owns what

ThingOwned byBehaviour
Application intake, approval, rejectionWebsite Do it on the website. The CRM receives the result.
PaymentBoth Record it in either system; it appears in the other within seconds.
CertificateBoth Issue in either; the number and link reach the other.
Dispatch & trackingBoth Update in either; courier, tracking and delivery mirror across.
Public awardee page, verification pageWebsite Served from the website. The CRM links to it, never replaces it.
Verification queue, tasks, notes, sales, reportsCRM Internal only. Never sent to the website.
Applicants created directly in the CRMCRM Stay in the CRM. They are never pushed to the public site.

Records you create in the CRM do not appear on the website. That is intentional. Use New Applicant in the CRM for walk-ins, offline entries and historic record-holders you are backfilling. If a person needs a public profile and a public certificate page, their application has to originate on the website.

02 Signing in

The CRM is staff-only and every action is written to an audit log against your name.

  1. Open the CRM address your administrator gave you and sign in with your work email or employee ID plus your password.
  2. If this is your first login, you are sent straight to Set your password. Nothing else in the system opens until you change it — the temporary password you were issued is treated as compromised.
  3. Changing your password signs you out everywhere, including the browser you are using. Sign in again with the new one.
Things worth knowing
  • Five wrong passwords locks the account for 15 minutes. Wait it out or ask an administrator.
  • Sessions end after 30 minutes idle. You will be returned to the sign-in screen; nothing is lost.
  • Forgot password emails a link valid for 30 minutes and usable once. The confirmation message is the same whether or not the address exists, so it cannot be used to discover who has an account.

03 Dashboard

/dashboard — where the day starts.

Counts across the pipeline, what is overdue, and recent activity. Every tile is a link: clicking a count opens the queue behind it already filtered. Use it to decide what to work on, not as a report — Reports is for numbers you intend to quote.

04 Applicants

/applicants — every person in the system, whether they came from the website or were added here.

The list

  • Search by name, mobile, email, applicant ID or record number.
  • Filter by status, category and date. Filters are held in the address bar, so a filtered list can be copied to a colleague and it opens the same way for them.
  • The three-dot menu at the end of each row holds that row's actions — open, edit, add a note, change status. Actions you lack permission for are greyed out with the reason on hover.

The global search box in the top bar (or ⌘K / Ctrl K) jumps straight to a person from anywhere in the system.

New Applicant

/applicants/new. Use this for a walk-in, a phone enquiry that is ready to become an application, or a historic record-holder being entered after the fact.

  1. Fill in the applicant's details. Name and mobile are required; everything else can follow later.
  2. Fill in the record: title, category, description, date achieved, location.
  3. Tick "This record was already awarded" for a back-entry, and supply the existing record number and certificate date. This skips the pipeline rather than pretending the record is new.
  4. Save & open profile. The system checks for a duplicate on mobile and warns you before creating a second file for the same person.

The category list here is the website's category list, kept in step automatically. If a category you expect is missing, it was retired on the website — add it there, not here.

05 The applicant profile

/applicants/:id — the screen you will spend most of the day on. One person, all their records, every tab.

The header

Name, applicant ID, contact details and six status chips — Application, Payment, Certificate, Publication, Dispatch, Blacklist — so the state of the whole file is readable without opening a tab. Edit, Export PDF and New record sit top-right. A person may hold several records; the record selector below the header switches between them.

Smart Actions

The panel on the right offers only what makes sense for the record's current status, so you are never choosing from a list of things that would fail. At Selected, for example:

Send selection email

Sends the selection template to the applicant and logs it on the timeline.

Send selection WhatsApp

Opens WhatsApp with the message prefilled; mark it sent to record it.

Download selection letter

Generates the PDF letter on official letterhead.

Set payment deadline

Starts the clock and schedules the reminders.

Add internal note

Staff-only. Never visible to the applicant.

Change status manually

For when the normal route does not fit. Asks for a reason.

The pipeline, in order

New Lead→ Application Submitted→ Under Review→ Verification Pending→ Selected→ Payment Pending→ Payment Received→ Certificate Pending→ Certificate Uploaded→ Publication→ Dispatch Pending→ Dispatched→ Delivered→ Completed

On Hold, Rejected and Closed sit outside the line and can be reached from most points. A record that arrives from the website lands on Selected — approved, awaiting money.

Statuses move forward, not backward. If you genuinely need to step a record back, use Change status manually and give a reason. The reason is recorded against your name and shows on the timeline.

07 Sales & leads

Outbound calling. This is separate from the applicant pipeline — these are people being called to invite them to apply.

Leads /leads

One row per person being called. The single filter bar combines status, owner, follow-up window and search, so you are not reconciling four controls.

Lead statuses

New Contacted Interested Callback Not interested Unqualified Converted Lost

Logging a call
  1. Open the lead, or use Log call from the row's three-dot menu.
  2. Pick the outcome: connected, no answer, busy, wrong number, switched off, callback requested, interested, not interested.
  3. Enter the duration and a summary of what was said.
  4. Set a follow-up date if you promised to call back. This creates a task automatically — the promise is not left in a text field where it will be missed.
  5. Save. The call joins the lead's call history with your name and the time.

The lead detail view shows every previous call — date, caller, outcome, duration and summary — so whoever picks up the phone next knows what was already said.

Converting a lead creates the applicant and record, and carries the entire call history onto the new applicant's timeline with the original dates and callers. Nothing is stranded on a closed lead.

Sales Dashboard /sales

Per-person figures for a chosen day: calls made, connected, minutes on the phone, interested, new leads, converted, and follow-ups missed. Below it, the pipeline by stage.

Send report now emails the same figures immediately. The same report goes out automatically each evening at the time set in Settings. The numbers in the email and the numbers on this screen come from the same place, so they always agree.

08 Tasks, blacklist and reports

Tasks & Follow-ups /tasks

A board of everything assigned, grouped by due date, with overdue first. Tasks arrive here from three places: created by hand, generated when you promise a follow-up on a call, and raised by the system when something needs attention. Assign, re-assign, change the due date, or complete.

Blacklist /blacklist

People or organisations who must not be processed. Add an entry with the reason and supporting detail. Once listed, their file shows a clear warning at the top and the pipeline actions are blocked. Lift removes the block and asks for a reason; the original entry is kept, not deleted.

Reports /reports

Applications over time, conversion, revenue, category mix, dispatch performance, staff activity. Choose a date range and export to CSV or PDF. These are the figures to quote externally — the dashboard is for triage.

09 Employees

/employees — the staff directory.

Employee code, name, designation, department, reporting manager, work email, personal contact, date of joining and status. Add, edit and deactivate from the three-dot menu on each row.

An employee is not a login. This directory is a record of who works here. Access to the CRM is granted separately under Users & Roles. Someone can appear here without an account, and deactivating them here does not revoke their access — do that in Users & Roles.

10 Administration

Users & Roles /admin/users

Create an account, assign a role, reset a password, deactivate. A new user is issued a temporary password and forced to change it at first login. Deactivating revokes their sessions immediately.

Templates /admin/templates

Email and WhatsApp templates. System templates that drive the pipeline can be edited but not deleted, so an automatic message can never be left with nothing to send. Custom templates can be created and deleted freely. Placeholders such as the applicant's name and record number are substituted when sent.

Settings /admin/settings

Organisation details, packages, categories, couriers, statuses, the evening sales report time and its recipients, and the DPDP consent notice.

Packages and categories are mirrored from the website and should be changed there, not here. Editing them locally puts a price or a category in front of an operator that the website will reject.

Audit Logs /admin/audit

Who did what, when, from where. Filterable by user, module, action and date. Append-only — entries cannot be edited or removed by anyone, including a Super Admin. Viewing an unmasked Aadhaar or passport number is itself an audited event.

Settings → Internal CRM, in detail

  • Test connection — sends a signed test push and reports exactly what came back. If the secrets differ it says so, and shows a fingerprint of each so you can compare without revealing either.
  • Backfill — sends every approved-and-beyond application that the CRM has not seen. Safe to run repeatedly; applications already there are updated, not duplicated.
  • Re-sync this application — on a single application, for when one record looks out of step.
  • Sync log — the last events in both directions with their outcome. This is the first place to look when something has not arrived.

12 What syncs, and when

EventDirectionResult
Application approved on the websiteWebsite → CRM Applicant and record created, status Selected, consent and evidence carried across.
Payment recorded in the CRMCRM → Website Payment appears on the website; the application moves to paid.
Payment recorded on the websiteWebsite → CRM Payment banked against the record, matched to the right package.
Certificate issued in either systemBoth ways Number and issue date mirrored; the public verification page stays authoritative.
Dispatch updated in either systemBoth ways Courier, tracking number, tracking link and delivery date mirrored.
Status changed in the CRMCRM only Internal statuses such as Completed have no counterpart on the website and are not sent.
Notes, tasks, leads, callsCRM only Never leave the CRM.

You cannot break it by doing the same thing twice. Recording the same payment in both systems does not double-count it, and re-sending a record does not create a duplicate. The two systems compare content and settle on the same answer.

Sync is normally a matter of seconds. If the receiving system is briefly down the sender retries; if it still fails, the failure is written to the sync log with the reason rather than disappearing.

13 When something looks wrong

What you seeAlmost always becauseDo this
An application is not in the CRM It has not been approved on the website yet. Check its status there. Approve it, and it arrives within seconds.
A CRM-created applicant is not on the website Working as intended — CRM records are internal. Nothing. If it must be public, it has to start on the website.
A payment shows in one system only A sync failure was recorded. Website → Settings → Internal CRM → Sync log. Then Re-sync this application.
"Signature could not be verified" The shared secret differs between the two systems. Ask an administrator to compare the fingerprints on the Internal CRM tab.
A category is missing when adding a record It is retired, or not on the website's list. Add it on the website; the CRM picks it up on the next sync.
A package price looks wrong Prices come from the website and are quoted all-inclusive. Change the price on the website, then re-sync packages.
A button is greyed out Your role does not allow it, or the record's status does not. Hover for the reason. Ask an administrator if you need the permission.
Signed out unexpectedly 30 minutes idle, or someone changed your password. Sign in again.

Never share your login. Every action is recorded against the account that performed it, including who viewed an applicant's Aadhaar or passport number. A shared account makes that record meaningless and puts the organisation's DPDP obligations at risk.
