How to read this tracker

The plan's 31 delivery tasks (P1-01…P1-15, P2-01…P2-16) are listed verbatim below, plus a DPDP Act compliance track that the requirement documents imply but do not enumerate. Everything marked Done has been exercised against a live Postgres + Redis stack — the evidence section lists what was actually run.

The current shape of the build is deliberate: the foundation is deep rather than wide. Database, security, workflow engine and privacy layer are production-grade; the screens on top of them are the remaining work. Building it the other way round produces a demo that cannot be hardened later without a rewrite.

India DPDP Act, 2023 — Compliance Track

NBR is the Data Fiduciary; applicants are Data Principals. This track is not in the original plan — it was added because the system stores Aadhaar numbers, minors' data and permanent personal history, which brings it squarely inside the Act. These are engineering controls, not legal advice — a lawyer should review before go-live.
